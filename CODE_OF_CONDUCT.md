# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Product Demo Director to be a welcoming, useful, and harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical confidence.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Respect different viewpoints and creative choices.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private information found in recordings, logs, and repository history.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone
represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue a
warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/crimeacs). Reports will be handled as privately and
fairly as possible. Do not open a public issue about another person's conduct.
